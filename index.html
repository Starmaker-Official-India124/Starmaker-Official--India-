How to Identify StarMaker Officials - India

★

Learn how to identify an official employee, communicate safely and protect your account.

Protect Yourself from Scammers and Hackers

Always verify a person's identity through trusted StarMaker channels before treating them as staff or support.

Important safety rules:

  • ★
    Never share your password, OTP, email code or recovery code.
  • ★
    Do not open suspicious links sent by unknown people.
  • ★
    Do not send money for promised account features or special status.
  • ★
    Use official in-app support for account problems.

How to Identify an Official Employee
★ Look for the “Official Employee” Tag (Blue V)

The blue V (Official) tag is the official employee indicator. Genuine StarMaker officials may show this tag. However, sometimes the blue V is intentionally hidden on official profiles to prevent spam and unnecessary messages. Always confirm identity through trusted StarMaker channels.

★Blue V missing does not automatically mean the person is fake — it may be hidden on purpose. Always verify through official support.
★ Do Not Trust Profiles Without Proper Verification

If someone claims to represent StarMaker, do not share personal information or account credentials just because of a tag or name. They may be an agency leader, coin seller, family captain, project coordinator or another user who is not an official of the app. Always confirm through official channels.

How to Communicate with an Official?

To help support teams understand your issue quickly, send all relevant details in one clear message instead of only writing “hi” or “hello”.

How to Communicate Safely

When contacting support, provide only the information needed for your issue.

  • ★
    Your StarMaker ID/SID when requested through a trusted support channel.
  • ★
    A short description of the problem.
  • ★
    Relevant screenshots only when appropriate.
✕ Avoid
Sending your password, OTP or recovery code.
✓ Better
Explain the issue and use the official support process.
Red Flags: Common Scams
  1. 1
    “Open this link to get free coins or rewards.”
    Avoid unknown links and never enter credentials on suspicious pages.
  2. 2
    “Send me your OTP so I can verify your account.”
    Never share a one-time code with another person.
  3. 3
    “Pay me for a special account feature.”
    Verify offers through official channels before paying anyone.
  4. 4
    “Install this app to fix your account.”
    Do not install unknown software or give remote access to your device.
Strengthen Your Account Security
  • ★
    Use available security features.
    Keep account and recovery settings up to date.
  • ★
    Keep credentials private.
    Never give passwords or verification codes to others.
  • ★
    Be careful with links.
    Check the destination before entering any information.
  • ★
    Report suspicious activity.
    Use trusted in-app support when possible.
